Four rules decide every path

  • Only issues reach specialists. The router must rate a piece a problem or a request at 0.5 or more. P4 is a feeling, so only the router and tone read it.
  • An issue goes to every team the router rates 0.3 or more. An account problem also goes to bug, so P2's login failure reaches both account and bug.
  • The linker reads every pair of issue pieces. Three issues make three pairs.
  • Rules merge piece answers one question at a time. Specialist answers take the strongest piece. Tone takes the angriest piece, the average mood and the rudest piece. Router answers come from each piece's kind and team chances, and 7 combined answers are built from other answers.
Five of these answers are walked through step by step in how_it_works.html, along with how the models were made and trained. Held-out accuracy is measured on 96 synthetic tickets the models never saw. The baseline always gives the most common answer. Timings come from models/eval.json, and every other number comes from a fresh run of the sample ticket.

How this page was made

  • I ran python3 -m micro_llm.resolve, then python3 -m micro_llm.resolve --thread 1 --account A-1001 "I confirmed it's me." twice, on 2026-10-25 at 17:52 UTC.
  • A traced replay of both messages recorded every model's inputs and outputs. Its database rows matched the CLI run's exactly, apart from timestamps.
  • Timings come from that replay, after one warm-up run, so they vary by a millisecond or two between runs.
  • The tools are stand-ins. Accounts, orders, charges and incidents come from examples/support_ticket_98423/systems.json, and every name in it is made up.
